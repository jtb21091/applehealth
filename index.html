Apple Health Data Visualizer

Explore numeric measurements from your Apple Health export. Your health file stays in this browser; it is never uploaded.

1. Open your export

In Health on your iPhone, tap your profile → Export All Health Data. Unzip the download and select apple_health_export/export.xml below.

Select export.xml to begin.

Large files are read in chunks in a background worker. Available memory still limits the number of measurements a device can hold.

2. Explore measurements

Dates use your browser’s local timezone and include the entire end date. Statistics use every matching record; the graph shows up to 4,000 representative points. Mean and ±2 standard deviation lines describe your data; they are not clinical thresholds.
